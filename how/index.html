How It Works

The Game Bureau makes walking tours that play like games. Real places, real stories, no app required.

Route Flow

From link to finish line.

Open the game, travel to the start, and follow clues that are written against the place in front of you.

5 Steps

01
Pick a game Browse the games and choose a city or story. Each route has its own starting point, tone, and rules.
02
Travel to the start Open the game on your phone and head to the rendezvous point. The route begins where the story needs you.
03
Walk and play Stops unfold one at a time with clues, photos, questions, and choices on the street.
04
Pay if prompted Free games are free. Paid games let you start free, then prompt you mid-game to continue.
05
Finish the story Reach the final stop, close out the game, and walk away with a new sense of the place.

Comfortable shoes. Charged phone. Street awareness.

Safety

The street outranks the screen. Stop in a safe place before reading clues, answering prompts, crossing streets, or taking photos.

Access

Nothing to install. Games run in the browser, so you can open the link and let the route take over from there.

Payment

No ticket booth. When a paid game asks you to unlock the rest, checkout returns you to the same spot in the route.
