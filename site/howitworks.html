How It Works

The Game Bureau makes walking tours that play like games. Real places, real stories, no app required.

  1. Pick a game

    Browse the library and choose a city or story. Each game has its own starting point, tone, and rules.

  2. Travel to the start

    Open the game on your phone and head to the rendezvous point. The route begins where the story needs you.

  3. Walk and play

    Stops unfold one at a time with clues, photos, questions, and choices on the street. Keep your head up and let the city answer.

  4. Pay if prompted

    Free games are free. Paid games let you start free, then prompt you mid-game to continue. Checkout is quick and secure.

  5. Finish the story

    Reach the final stop and close out the game. Walk away with a new sense of the place and probably a new favorite spot.

What to expect

Bring a charged phone, comfortable shoes, and enough attention for traffic, weather, crowds, and whatever the city is doing that day.

Before you step out

A few Bureau rules.

Safety

The street outranks the screen

Stop in a safe place before reading clues, answering prompts, crossing streets, or taking photos.

Access

Nothing to install

Games run in the browser. Open the link, travel to the start, and the route takes over from there.

Payment

No ticket booth

When a paid game asks you to unlock the rest, payment happens inside the route and returns you to the same spot.
