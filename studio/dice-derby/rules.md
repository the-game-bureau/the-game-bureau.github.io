# Dice Derby rules

You need this notebook, two six-sided dice, and a pencil. Play with 2 to 6 owners. A full meet is 24 races, or play as many as you like.

## Before each race

1. **Pick a race card.** Use the next empty race in the notebook.
2. **Draft the horses.** The owner with the fewest points picks first (roll a die to break a tie). Going around the table, each owner claims one horse at a time by writing their initials in its **OWNER** box. Keep drafting until every owner has the same number of horses.
3. **Leftover horses run anyway.** Any horse nobody owns still races. If it wins, nobody scores.

## Running the race

1. **Roll both dice.** Add them up. The horse with that number moves.
2. **Move it one box.** Fill in the next empty box in that horse's lane, starting from the gate.
3. **Pass the dice.** Owners take turns rolling. It doesn't matter who rolls: every roll moves a horse.
4. **First past the post.** The first horse to fill the last box in its lane wins.

## Scoring

The winner's owner scores the **PAYS** number printed at the end of the lane. Write it in the **Stable Book** under that race.


- **7** has 8 boxes and pays **1**. It runs the most.
- **6 and 8** have 7 boxes and pay **2**.
- **5 and 9** have 6 boxes and pay **2**.
- **4 and 10** have 5 boxes and pay **3**.
- **3 and 11** have 3 boxes and pay **4**.
- **2 and 12** have 2 boxes and pay **6**. Long shots.

> **Photo finish.** Only one horse moves per roll, so there are no ties in a race. If the meet ends tied on points, the tied owners each roll two dice. High total wins.

## Winning the meet

After the last race, add up each owner's points in the Stable Book. The most points wins the meet.

## House rules

Agree on these before the first race if you want them.

- **Scratch.** Before the first roll, an owner may scratch one of their horses. Cross out its lane. If its number is rolled, roll again.
- **Show money.** The horse in second place when the race ends pays 1 point to its owner.
