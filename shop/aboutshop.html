How Our Shop Works

Every pick in the shop is tied to a city one of our games is played in — a book worth reading before you go, a keepsake from the route, something free to help you find your way. We don't hold inventory or mark anything up; you always check out with the retailer or open the publication directly. The picks come from three places.

  • Bookshop.org — our preferred source for books. Buying here supports independent bookstores instead of a warehouse, so it's what we lead with whenever a title's involved.
  • Amazon — for the non-book finds (gear, gadgets, oddities) and the occasional title Bookshop can't carry, through the Amazon Associates program.
  • Free publications — hand-picked, no-cost travel guides, self-guided audio tours, and free walking tours, delivered as PDFs, web pages, or audio. No affiliate, no catch.

Why we lead with Bookshop.org

Bookshop.org exists to give independent bookstores a fighting chance online. It's a certified B Corporation, and it has already channeled tens of millions of dollars to local shops. Buy a book through our Bookshop links and a share goes into a pool that's split among independent bookstores — the same shops that host the readings, hand-sell the weird stuff, and keep a neighborhood's literary pulse going.

Amazon is convenient, and we use it. But convenience has quietly hollowed out Main Street bookshops for two decades. Bookshop.org is the rare alternative that's just as easy to click and puts money back where books actually come alive — so whenever a title exists on both, we point you at Bookshop first. You can read their mission here.

What it costs you

Nothing extra, ever. You pay the retailer's normal price — the small referral on Amazon and Bookshop purchases is paid out of their end, not added to yours. The free publications carry no affiliate tag and no cost at all; they're here purely because they're worth your time. On the shop grid, the "Curated from" filter lets you show only the sources you want.
