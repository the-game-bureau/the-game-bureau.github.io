Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

The Game Bureau makes walking games you play in real cities. This page says exactly what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how to have it deleted. It describes what our software actually does, not what a template says it might.

The short version. Browsing this site is anonymous — we use a cookieless analytics tag that cannot identify you and sets nothing on your device. We only hold personal information if you buy a game or play one. We do not sell data, we run no advertising trackers, and we do not build profiles.

Browsing the site

Our analytics is Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is cookieless and stores no per-visitor identifier, so it can tell us that a page was viewed but never that you viewed it, and it cannot follow you to other sites. That is why this site shows no cookie banner: there is no tracking cookie to consent to.

Our own admin pages are excluded from it, so our work does not pollute the figures.

Things loaded from other companies

Some pages load resources from third parties, which necessarily see your IP address because that is how the web works:

  • Google Fonts — typefaces.
  • Spotify — the players on our city soundtrack pages. A Spotify embed may set its own cookies; that is governed by Spotify's privacy policy, not ours.
  • YouTube — video embeds, using the youtube-nocookie domain where we can.
  • Supabase — our database, which the pages read directly.

Buying a game

Payments are taken by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never touch our servers — we never see or store a card number.

From a purchase we keep:

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo send the access code and to find your purchase if you write to us.
Team name and team leader nameYou choose these at checkout; they appear in the game and on the Winner's Wall.
Recipient name and email, if you send a game as a giftTo deliver the code to them.
The access code and which game it unlocksTo let it be redeemed, once.

Access-code emails are delivered by Resend. We send no marketing email and run no mailing list, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from.

Playing a game

While a team plays, the game records progress so we can show results and see which puzzles are too hard:

  • A randomly generated play id, created in your browser. It identifies the playthrough, not the person.
  • Your team name and team leader name, and the answers your team types in.
  • Progress events — which stop you reached and when.
  • Photos your team chooses to submit at a stop.

Because the team name and answers are typed by you, please do not put anything in them you would not want us to hold. Winning teams appear on the Winner's Wall by team name.

Photos. Photos submitted during a game are stored in our database and may be shown on the Winner's Wall or our social accounts. If you would rather a photo were not used, or want one removed after the fact, email us and we will delete it.

Children

Our games are family-friendly and children often play as part of a team, but the account and the purchase belong to an adult. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child. If you believe a child has sent us something, write to us and we will remove it.

Who else sees your information

Only the companies that make the product work, and only for that purpose:

  • Stripe — payments.
  • Supabase — our database and file storage.
  • Resend — sending access-code emails.
  • Cloudflare — anonymous page-view counts.
  • GitHub — hosting for the site's files.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. We would disclose it if the law required us to.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept while they may be needed for support, refunds and tax. Gameplay records are kept so results and the Winner's Wall stay meaningful. Anonymous analytics is aggregate and holds nothing about you. If you ask us to delete your information, we will, except where we are legally required to keep a record of a transaction.

Getting your information, or having it deleted

Email privacy@thegamebureau.com and tell us what you want: a copy of what we hold, a correction, or deletion. Use the email address you bought with, or tell us the team name you played under, so we can find the right records. We aim to reply within 30 days.

This applies to everyone. We do not think the right to see or delete your own information should depend on which country you live in.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top. There is no mailing list to notify, so the date is the honest signal.
