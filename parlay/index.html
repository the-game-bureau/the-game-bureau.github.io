RULES

Before kickoff

  1. Plan the game. Play with 2 to 12 people. Choose to fund 12, 24, 36, or 48 drives.
  2. Assign all 12 PROG #s. Each number costs the same. A person can own more than one.
  3. Pay up front. Pay for your numbers once to fill the pot. Write each owner's name on the Prognosticators page. Keep those numbers all game.

Each drive

  1. Claim a square. Follow the printed pick order. Each PROG # gets one pick. Write that number in an open square.
  2. Close picks. Finish before the kickoff or snap that starts the drive.
  3. Find the winner. When the drive ends, write the result number in the matching square's tab. Its owner wins that drive's payout.
  4. End the game. Play until the pot runs out or the football game ends. Split any leftover money evenly.

Pick order. Start with #1 through #12, then reverse on the next drive. Start each new pair one number higher and wrap after #12.

Find the matching square

Touchdown

Run or pass. Use the scorer's jersey number to decide odd or even.

Field goal

Good or miss. Use the official kick distance in yards to decide odd or even.

A block counts as a miss. If a blocked kick is returned for a touchdown, use Touchdown Run and the returner's jersey number.

Turn over

Punt or other. Use this group if there's no touchdown or field goal attempt. Other includes interceptions, lost fumbles, turnovers on downs, safeties, and the end of the half or game.

Use the last two digits on the game clock when the drive ends. 00 is even.

Unusual plays. Before kickoff, agree how to handle fake punts and kicks, penalties, and other defensive touchdowns.
