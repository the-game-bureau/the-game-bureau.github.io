How it works

All you need is the Parlay Squares Notebook and a pen.

1

Buy in before kickoff

Between 2 and 12 prognosticators ante up for the entire game.

2

Claim a square before every drive

Before each drive starts, every prognosticator, in the listed order, picks one of 12 different parlays predicting how the drive will end.

3

The drive picks the winner

When the drive ends, exactly one parlay square matches. Note the winner for later payout.
