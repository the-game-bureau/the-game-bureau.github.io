Parlay Squares print admin

Makes the two PDFs Lulu needs. Each button opens the page and starts printing.

Cover

One 17.25 x 11.25 in wraparound spread: back on the left, front on the right, 0.125 in bleed.

Print cover

Workbook (interior)

29 pages at 8.75 x 11.25 in (8.5 x 11 trim plus 0.125 in bleed). Starts at Rules.

Print workbook
  1. In the print dialog choose Save as PDF.
  2. Set Margins: Default, turn Background graphics on, and turn headers and footers off.
  3. Upload the cover PDF and the workbook PDF to Lulu separately.
