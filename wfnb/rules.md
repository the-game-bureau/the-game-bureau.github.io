# {{title}} rules

**2 captains · 15–30 minutes · Ages 10+**

Hide five word-ships on your Battle Tracker. Guess a whole word in the opposing fleet to earn a shot. To win, name and locate all five opposing word-ships.

## Get your Battle Tracker ready

1. **Take a sheet.** Each captain tears one Battle Tracker out of the notebook or uses a printed copy.
2. **Name your fleet.** Write its name on the **Fleet Name** line.
3. **Keep your sheet private.** Shield your word list, Defense Grid, and five Launch Codes from the other captain.

### Know your Battle Tracker

- **Attack Grid.** Mark your shots and write in the letters you uncover.
- **Attack Manifest.** Record the letter tallies your opponent gives you.
- **Defense Grid.** Write your own word-ships here.
- **Defense Manifest.** Look up how many times a letter appears in your fleet.
- **Launch Codes.** Use these five secret numbers to bluff for the first turn.

## Deploy your word-ships

1. **Find your fleet.** Use the five words printed above your Defense Grid. Their lengths are 5, 4, 3, 3, and 2 letters.
2. **Place each word.** Write one letter per square, either left to right across a row or top to bottom down a column.
3. **Check your placement.** Keep every letter inside the grid. Ships may touch, but they cannot share a square.

> **Example.** To place MAP across row 4, write M in C4, A in D4, and P in E4.

## Decide who goes first

1. **Call a launch code.** Either captain can call any three-digit number from 000 through 999. Use a code printed on your sheet or bluff.
2. **Respond.** The other captain chooses one response.

   - **Concede.** The caller takes the first turn.
   - **Challenge.** The caller must show that the code appears on their sheet. If it does, the caller goes first. If it was a bluff, the challenger goes first.
   - **Counter.** Call a code equal to or higher than the last one. You become the caller, and the other captain responds.

3. **Keep going** until a concession or challenge settles who goes first.

> **Example.** You call 850. Your opponent counters with 900. You challenge. If 900 is missing from their sheet, you go first.

## Take a turn

Alternate turns. At the start of your turn, choose whether to attack or demand surrender.

### Attack

1. **Guess a whole word.** Say one word you think is in your opponent’s fleet. Your opponent checks their word list. If you’re right, take one shot. If you’re wrong, pass the turn.
2. **Call a square.** Say a column letter from A to J and a row number from 1 to 10, such as E7. Your opponent checks that square on their Defense Grid.

   - **Miss.** Put a dot in that square on your Attack Grid and pass the turn.
   - **Hit.** Outline that square on your Attack Grid and guess one letter.

3. **Mark the result of your letter guess.**

   - **Correct letter.** Your opponent confirms it. Write the letter in that square on your Attack Grid.
   - **Wrong letter.** Your opponent checks their Defense Manifest and tells you how many times your guessed letter appears in their entire fleet, including any you’ve already found. Write the tally in your Attack Manifest, even if it’s zero.

4. **Pass the turn.**

> **Tip.** On a later turn, you can earn another shot and fire at a square you’ve already hit to try a different letter. If you guess A and your opponent says it appears twice, write 2 in the A box of your Attack Manifest.

### Demand surrender

1. **Call the entire fleet.** At the start of your turn, you may demand surrender instead of attacking. Say all five opposing words. For each one, spell it out and give the first and last coordinates in spelling order.
2. **Check the fleet.** Your opponent waits until you’ve called all five, then checks each word, spelling, and location against their Defense Grid.
3. **Declare the winner.** If every word, spelling, and location is correct, you win immediately. If any part is wrong, your opponent wins immediately.

> **Example.** Say “MAP, M-A-P, from C4 to E4,” then call the other four word-ships the same way.
