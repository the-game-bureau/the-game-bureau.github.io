What This Is

Not a booking engine. Not a brochure. A playable layer over the city.

The Game Bureau takes tours, scavenger hunts, takeovers, and local lore and gives them a cleaner operating system. You choose a route in the Library, play the city in the field, and end with reading-room style objects in Gift Shop.

“The antidote to the walking tour” should feel like a category, not a tagline.
