Overview Builder Mapper

Build a game's route from waypoints. Pick the game, check the stops to include, order them, mark the end, and save.

  1. Pick a game — choose it from the list; any saved route loads automatically.
  2. Add stops — every waypoint shows as an orange dot on the map; click a dot to add it (or filter by city and check them in the list).
  3. Order & finish — reorder with ▲▼, mark one stop as End, then Save map.
