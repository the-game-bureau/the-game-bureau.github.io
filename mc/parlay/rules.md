# Parlay Squares

One winner every drive. Twelve squares, twelve picks, one square wins.

## GAME PLAN

Before each drive, PROGNOSTICATORS each claim a different square. Every square
is a three-part guess about how the drive will end. When the drive is over, find
the square that matches what happened. Whoever owns the square wins that drive.

## Before the game

1. **Choose your players.** Play with anywhere from 2 to 12 **PROGNOSTICATORS**.
2. **Agree on the length.** Play 12, 24, 36, or 48 drives. Decide before kickoff.
3. **Buy the PROG #s.** There are always twelve PROG #s (1 to 12), and every one
   must be taken. Each PROG # costs the same. One person can buy more than one
   PROG #, so with fewer than 12 players some people will own several.
4. **Pay up front.** Everyone pays for their PROG #s before the game starts. There
   are no per-drive antes. Each drive's pot is already funded.
5. **Write it down.** On the PROGNOSTICATORS page, write each person's name beside
   every PROG # they own. PROG #s stay with the same person all game.

A PROG # is a seat at the table, not a player's jersey number.

## Every drive

1. **Pick.** All twelve PROG #s pick, one square each, and no two can take the
   same square. Write the PROG # in the square it claims.
2. **Follow the printed order.** The pick order snakes:
   - Drive 1: #1 through #12.
   - Drive 2: #12 back to #1.
   - Then move the starting PROG # up by one and snake forward and back again.

   Always use the printed twelve-pick order, even with fewer than twelve people.
   If you own several PROG #s, you take a turn each time one of yours comes up.
3. **Stop picking at the start of the possession.** Picks close before the kickoff
   or snap that starts the drive.
4. **Settle.** When the drive ends, mark the square that matches what happened. The
   person who owns the PROG # in that square wins the drive's pot.

## The 12 squares

Every square answers three questions: **how did the drive end**, **which of two
ways**, and **odd or even**.

### Touchdown (4 squares)

| | Odd jersey | Even jersey |
|---|---|---|
| **Run** | TD · RUN · ODD | TD · RUN · EVEN |
| **Pass** | TD · PASS · ODD | TD · PASS · EVEN |

- **Run or pass:** how the touchdown was scored.
- **Odd or even:** the JERSEY NUMBER of the player who scored.

### Field goal (4 squares)

| | Odd yards | Even yards |
|---|---|---|
| **Good** | FG · GOOD · ODD | FG · GOOD · EVEN |
| **Miss** | FG · MISS · ODD | FG · MISS · EVEN |

- **Good or miss:** whether the attempt went through.
- **Odd or even:** the official KICK YARDS of the attempt.
- **Blocked kick:** counts as MISS.
- **Blocked kick returned for a touchdown:** counts as TOUCHDOWN · RUN, not field
  goal. Use the JERSEY NUMBER of the player who scored.

### Turn over (4 squares)

Turn over means the drive ended without a touchdown or a field goal attempt:
a punt, an interception, a fumble, a turnover on downs, a safety, or time running
out. A safety does put points on the board, which is why this group is called Turn
over and not "no score."

| | Odd game clock | Even game clock |
|---|---|---|
| **Punt** | TO · PUNT · ODD | TO · PUNT · EVEN |
| **Other** | TO · OTHER · ODD | TO · OTHER · EVEN |

- **Punt:** the drive ended with a punt.
- **Other:** the drive ended any other way:
  - an interception or lost fumble,
  - a turnover on downs,
  - a safety,
  - the half or the game ran out of time.
- **Odd or even:** the seconds on the GAME CLOCK (the last two digits, 00 to 59)
  when the drive ends. 00 counts as EVEN.

## Unusual plays

The rules above cover most drives. For anything else, such as fake punts or fake
field goals, defensive touchdowns other than a blocked field-goal return, or
penalties, agree on how you'll rule before the game starts and stick to it.
