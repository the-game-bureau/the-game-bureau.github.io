What this is

Five things worth posting

Every morning an AI agent reads around and brings back five stories or posts The Game Bureau might want to share. It works a beat tilted at games and travel — puzzle hunts, scavenger hunts and geocaching, odd competitions, first-person travel writing, plus tourism, weird stuff, sports culture, TV and music. A story does not have to be about a city we sell into, or about a city at all. It writes the caption for each one, tags what the story is about, and says which of our four accounts it suits and why — based on what media the story actually has.

Your job is the two buttons. COPY puts the blurb and the link on your clipboard, ready to paste wherever you are posting — and each platform tag links straight to our account there. TRASH clears one you do not want, and remembers — it will not come back on reload.

Nothing here posts itself. The agent has no access to any account. It only ever writes this list; a person decides what actually goes out.

Each run appends its picks, so the list builds up over the day rather than being wiped. Trash the ones you are done with.
