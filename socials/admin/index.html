What this is

Five things worth posting

Every morning an AI agent reads around and brings back five stories or posts The Game Bureau might want to share. It works a beat: travel and tourism, travel stories, competition, weird stuff, sports, TV, music, and our own genre of scavenger hunts and city games. It writes the caption for each one, tags what the story is about, and says which of our three accounts it suits and why — based on what media the story actually has.

Your job is the two buttons. POST copies the blurb and the link to your clipboard, ready to paste wherever you are posting. TRASH clears one you do not want, and remembers — it will not come back on reload.

Nothing here posts itself. The agent has no access to any account. It only ever writes this list; a person decides what actually goes out.

The list is replaced wholesale every morning, so an unposted pick is gone tomorrow. If you want one, take it today.

House style

What makes a good post

Curious, specific, dry. Lead with the interesting thing. Name the city. We sound like a well-travelled friend pointing at something, not a brand chasing engagement.

No hashtags, no emoji, no exclamation marks, no “link in bio”. Nothing political, no tragedy, no SEO listicles.

Edit any blurb after pasting — what the clipboard gives you is a starting point, not a rule.
